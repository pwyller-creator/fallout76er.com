Mire Map 05 — The Bare Earth Crossing

Region: The Mire · Map: 05 · Status: Documented · Threat: High

From wherever you are, fast travel to Ella Ames' Bunker. From where it drops you, spin your view until you're facing east — you should see a stump directly in front of you. Head east over the stump and over 2 N-shaped tree roots — like the tree has a leg that bent 90 degrees at the knee. Continue straight east until you can see the elevated highway. The highway should essentially bisect your view from left to right, centered top to bottom. Dead center of your east-facing view should be where the terrain starts to drop away from the road. A few steps forward, and you should see a bare patch of earth running north to south. Examine the bare earth just north of where you first cross it — you will find the treasure mound. Super easy — unless you get terrorized by the Mothman too...
