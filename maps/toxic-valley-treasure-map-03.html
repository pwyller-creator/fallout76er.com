Dead Boyscouts Tell No Tales

Region: Toxic Valley · Map: 03 · Status: Documented · Threat: Low

Fast travel to the Pioneer Scout Camp on Grafton Lake — it's not the cheeriest merit-badge program the Boy Scouts of America ever ran, and this itinerary proves it. Head for one of the two wooden docks jutting into the lake; either one gets you to the little cluster of islands squatting in the middle of the water. You want the specific island off the eastern dock — the one still nursing a wrecked sailing skiff on its shore, presumably from the last scoutmaster who thought "capsizing drills" sounded like fun. Once you're standing on the wreck, it's a short scramble to the black pebble mound a few paces inland. Dig, and Mr. Handy's your uncle — assuming your uncle's a homicidal robot butler who just wants you to have nice things.
