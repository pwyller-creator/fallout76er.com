About Me

        
Hi, I'm Fallout76er, a fairly new traveller in the wasteland. I am always looking for a new plan I haven't learned, a super rare item, or a funny or hidden scene tucked away somewhere on the map. I am generally a solo player, but I'll tag along on a casual teams for the perks - this generally means I've not yet run raids. I enjoy camp building, but I am largely a pragmatic builder - I build working camps with as much panche as I can. Feel free to add me as a friend, if you like.

        
My vendor is mostly stocked with plans, rare items, and apparel for 50 caps - I try to be new player friendly, since I appreciated the players whose shops helped me when I was coming up. Find me as Fallout76er in Social drop me a howdy-doo... or a loot bag loaded with rare stuff!

Tips for New Wastelanders

        
Don't just stay in a dead team. If you need to get to a specific corner of the Mire or the far reaches of the Skyline Valley, pull up the Social menu. Check the map for player icons in those regions, see what teams they are on, and jump ship. Using teammates' CAMPs and Survival Tents as free anchors is the only way to move across Appalachia without draining your caps.

        
Daily and Weekly challenges shouldn't be a race against the clock or other players. If the challenge is "Kill 10 Yao Guai" or "Collect 50 Wood," don't fight over spawns at Dolly Sods in a public server. Pop into a Private World. Every spawn is fresh, every resource node is full, and you won't find a level 1000 player has already cleared out the lead at the Charleston Fire Department.

        
Since the map expanded, the Rusty Pick isn't the only game in town. Rosewood Park and many Blue Ridge Caravan spots offer free or heavily discounted travel. Additionally, if you’re overencumbered and miles from a stash, remember that starting an Expedition (even if you don't intend to finish it) will teleport you to a hub with a stash box and scrip machine, regardless of your weight.

        
Forget selling tin cans and straight jackets. Once you hit the endgame, your daily 1,400 caps should come from selling Mutation Serums to vendors. With the right perks, a single serum sells for 500 caps. Three sales and you’ve cleared the vendor for the day in thirty seconds, leaving you more time for more important things—like perfecting your legendary rolls.

        
Can't craft Mutation Serums? Equip the Scrapper perk and pop a Guns and Bullets #5 magazine to maximize the scrap yield from Treasure Maps, then sell the bulked materials or the heaviest weapons to hit your 1,400-cap vendor daily limit in one go. Park your Survival Tent mear the Treasure Map dig site for cheaper travel to and fro.

        
If you’re on a team and your teammates are standing near you while you dig treasure, they get a small share of the loot (mostly caps and some consumables). If you have friends at a similar level, rotate who digs at the treasure site. You’ll multiply the total caps the group brings in without spending any extra maps.

        
Keeping a localized "spawn list" is the ultimate pro move for knocking out Dailies in under ten minutes; for example, knowing exactly where to find 3 Snallygasters (Toxic Larry's) or 5 Yao Guai (Middle Mountain Pitstop) saves you from wandering aimlessly. Additionally, never skip Public Events, as they are the most efficient way to farm S.C.O.R.E. and season tickets while simultaneously stocking up on Treasury Notes and legendary scrip.

        
Moonshine Jamboree is arguably the best cap farm in the game. If you loot every gulper, you’ll walk away with hundreds of Gulper Innards. Cook them into Gulper Slurry (with Super Duper equipped) to gain massive XP and enough high-value food to clear out a vendor’s entire 1,400-cap stash in one go. It’s also one of the few reliable sources of Raider Reputation.

		
Safe and Sound is the "goodie" goldmine for high-level players looking for specific gear. It’s the primary source for the Blue Ridge Branding Iron and the Blue Ridge Scout Outfit, which still hold high trade value in player vendors. Beyond the XP from the waves of Cryptids, the event rewards Treasury Notes and often drops high-tier consumables like Stimpaks and Rad-Away that you can sell or hoard for your next run.

		
Don't ask me for help or tips on Raids - maybe when I reach level 1000.


        
Rusty's Curios & Rares

        
My camp vendor is open whenever I'm online. Stock rotates regularly — rare weapon plans, apparel, the occasional legendary. Look for the billboard on the Burning Springs riverbank, just off Route 50. You can't miss it. The Deathclaw nest out front is a feature, not a bug.


        
Mutations

        
Eight mutations locked in permanently by Starched Genes, with penalties cut ~75% by Class Freak rank 3. The set works as a system: Eagle Eyes and Adrenal Reaction fuel the crit loop; Egg Head feeds Intelligence back into the build; Grounded provides energy resistance without touching the weapon loadout; Marsupial and Speed Demon keep mobility and carry weight competitive; Herd Mentality rewards grouping; Well Hydrated sustains AP regen for the critical meter. The negatives are largely theoretical at this point.

Furious Prime Dom Pedro Rifle ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

          

            
          

          
The Dom Pedro is more than a repurposed frontier rifle; it is a relic of "The Magnanimous" philosophy, adapted for the harsh realities of the Appalachian Wasteland. Named after the last Emperor of Brazil—a scholar-king who valued progress but was ultimately forced into exile—this five-star Lever Action represents a similar marriage of tradition and radical advancement. Legend among seasoned Vault Dwellers suggests the rifle was commissioned by a high-ranking technician who grew tired of the "obscene" waste of heavy energy weapons. It does not belong to the trading posts or the caps-driven economy of the Whitespring; it is a non-transferable statement of personal sovereignty. To carry Dom Pedro is to wield a tool that demands perfection and returns it in kind.

Fallout 76 Wasteland Intel — Pip-Boy terminal covering weekly nuke codes for Site Alpha, Site Bravo, and Site Charlie; Minerva's Emporium location tracker; a gold bullion planner calculating how many days until you can afford any bullion plan from treasury notes and Smiley purchases; live Appalachia in-game clock showing current in-game time, date, and days since the bombs fell; and spawn location intel for daily and weekly challenges throughout Appalachia.

      
Fallout 76 nuke codes reset every Wednesday evening around 8PM ET, with new codes taking effect Thursday. Nuclear launch codes are required to access Sites Alpha, Bravo, and Charlie in the Savage Divide region of Appalachia.

      
The Fallout 76 gold bullion planner calculates how long it takes to afford a bullion plan: treasury notes convert at 10 gold bullion each at gold press machines with a limit of 400 bullion per day, Smiley at the Wayward sells up to 300 bullion per week at 20 caps apiece, and a character can hold at most 10,000 gold bullion.

      
The Fallout 76 damage stacker calculates real damage per shot from your Pip-Boy card damage: sneak attacks deal 2x base damage rising to 2.5x with Covert Operative rank 3 for ranged weapons or 3x with Ninja rank 2 for melee, bows, and thrown weapons; V.A.T.S. critical hits add 1x base damage rising to 2x with Better Criticals rank 3; and enemy damage resistance mitigates hits on a curve of 0.15 times damage over resistance to the power 0.365, reduced by armor penetration from Anti-Armor weapons at 50% and the Tank Killer perk at 20% or 40%.

      
Minerva's Emporium is a travelling vendor in Fallout 76 who visits Foundation, The Crater, Fort Atlas, and Whitespring Resort on a rotating schedule, offering legendary plans and rare items at reduced prices.

      
Looking for the best Fallout 76 spawn locations for daily and weekly challenges? This guide covers where to find every common challenge enemy in Appalachia.

      
Where to find Yao Guai in Fallout 76: The best Yao Guai spawn locations are Dolly Sods Wilderness in The Mire with two guaranteed spawns, Mountainside Bed and Breakfast in the Savage Divide with two reliable spawns along the railway tracks to the west, and High Knob Lookout in Skyline Valley with three guaranteed spawns northeast of the lookout.

      
Where to find Snallygasters in Fallout 76: The best Snallygaster spawn locations are the Flooded Trainyard in Cranberry Bog with up to seven spawns making it the single best farming location in Appalachia, Toxic Larry's Meat 'n Go in the Savage Divide with three guaranteed spawns outside the building, and Charleston Downtown in The Forest with two guaranteed spawns between Hornwright HQ and the Capitol Building.

      
Where to find Deathclaws in Fallout 76: The best Deathclaw spawn locations are Deathclaw Island in The Forest southeast of Vault 76 with one guaranteed spawn (level 21, approachable for newer survivors), Thunder Mountain Substation in Cranberry Bog with one guaranteed spawn that often appears mid-brawl with a pack of giant ants, and Abandoned Waste Dump in The Mire northeast of Harpers Ferry with two guaranteed spawns and a hidden bunker entrance behind it — the most reliable multi-Deathclaw stop in Appalachia.

      
Where to find Mole Miners in Fallout 76: The best Mole Miner spawn locations are Welch in the Ash Heap with up to ten spawns patrolling the town, Blackwater Mine in the Savage Divide with five to eight interior and exterior spawns, and Mount Blair Trainyard in the Ash Heap with three to five spawns across multiple pockets.

      
Where to find Feral Ghouls in Fallout 76: The best Feral Ghoul spawn locations are Watoga Emergency Services in Cranberry Bog with eight or more spawns crowding the top floor of the old Brotherhood field hospital, Charleston Fire Department in The Forest with six to ten spawns, and Grafton Steel in Toxic Valley with six to eight spawns.

      
Where to find Super Mutants in Fallout 76: The best Super Mutant spawn locations are West Tek Research Center in the Savage Divide with ten to fifteen interior spawns and a high legendary drop rate, Grafton Dam in Toxic Valley with six to eight spawns, and Huntersville in the Savage Divide with eight to twelve spawns across the entire town.

      
Where to find Scorched in Fallout 76: The best Scorched spawn locations are Monongah Power Plant in the Savage Divide with ten to twenty permanently infested spawns, Morgantown Airport in The Forest with eight to twelve spawns shared with Feral Ghouls, and Site Charlie in the Savage Divide with six to ten spawns that increase during nuke events.

      
Where to find Mirelurks in Fallout 76: The best Mirelurk spawn locations are Summersville in The Forest with up to ten spawns along the riverbank and inside the red-and-white house at the edge of town, Lakeside Cabins in The Forest with three to five spawns including Hunter variants, and Harpers Ferry in The Mire with four to seven spawns along the town exterior and riverbank.

      
Where to find Wendigos in Fallout 76: The best Wendigo spawn locations are Abbie's Bunker in The Mire with up to three spawns south of the bunker entrance by the truck suspended in the trees, Wendigo Cave in the Savage Divide with two to three interior spawns, and KMAX Transmission in The Mire with one to two consistent spawns.

      
Where to find Radstags in Fallout 76: The best Radstag spawn locations are Thomas Farm in the Savage Divide with four to six spawns from an Aerosolizer-bleached albino herd, Whitespring Golf Club in the Savage Divide with two to three spawns grazing the fairways near the clubhouse, and Gauley Mine in The Forest with two spawns along the railroad tracks at the mine exit.

      
Where to find Blood Eagles in Fallout 76: The best Blood Eagle spawn locations are Widow's Perch in the Ash Heap with four to six guaranteed spawns, The Sludge Works in the Ash Heap with five to eight spawns across the chemical plant, and Rollins Labor Camp in the Ash Heap with eight to twelve spawns — the single densest Blood Eagle camp in Appalachia.

      
Where to find Cryptids in Fallout 76: The best cryptid spawn locations for daily and weekly challenges are Toxic Larry's Meat 'n Go in the Savage Divide with three guaranteed Snallygasters, Abbie's Bunker in The Mire with up to three Wendigos, and the Flooded Trainyard in Cranberry Bog with up to seven Snallygasters.
