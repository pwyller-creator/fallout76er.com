Fallout 76 Pint-Sized Slasher Mask Locations — All 108, With Directions

Topic: The Slasher Update · Status: Field-Verified · Last Checked: 09.21.2026

The Slasher Update left Appalachia with 108 masked corpses, killed and dressed by the Pint-Sized Phantoms and parked at fixed spots, each one wearing a Pint-Sized Slasher Mask. The quest The Slasher: Masked Truth wants 10 of them. The four challenge tiers want 3, 10, 30 and 50 — 93 in all. Below is every one: 61 locations across 8 regions, how to reach each, and where the mask sits once you're standing there.

Where each mask sits was checked in game. The directions to reach each spot are worked out from the game's own map-marker coordinates — nearest landmark, compass direction, rough distance — so treat the distances as close enough to find it, not survey-grade. Get within earshot and listen for laughter.

Slasher Mask FAQ

How many Pint-Sized Slasher Masks are there in Fallout 76?

108 — one on each masked corpse, spread across 61 locations in 8 regions. Atlantic City has none, and the Cranberry Bog has exactly one (Ranger Lookout).

How many masks do I need for The Slasher: Masked Truth and the challenges?

The quest The Slasher: Masked Truth needs 10. The four challenge tiers need 3, 10, 30 and 50 — 93 in all — out of 108 available.

How do I know when I am close to a masked corpse?

Listen for laughter. The masked corpses, killed and dressed by the Pint-Sized Phantoms, sit at fixed spots, and you will hear laughter when you are close to one.

Which region has the most Pint-Sized Slasher Masks?

Savage Divide has the most: 32 masks across 20 locations. The Forest is next with 24 across 14.

Which locations hold three masks?

16 locations hold three masks each: Alpine River Cabins, Camp McClintock, Tyler County Fairgrounds, New River Gorge Resort, Dolly Sods Campground, Sunday Brothers' Cabin, Bastion Park, Spruce Knob Lake, Sunnytop Ski Lanes, Wavy Willard's Water Park, Pioneer Scout Camp, Camden Park, Nuka-World on Tour, Camp Liberty, Rapidan Camp and Dino Peaks Mini Golf. They are the most efficient stops on a mask run.

Are any of the mask spots missing from the in-game map?

5 of them had no map marker of their own in the game's marker data: Fissure Site Sigma, NW of Seneca Gang Camp, Trapper's Camp, Cobby's Corner and Strouds Run State Park. The directions for those use the nearest named landmark instead.

How were these locations and directions worked out?

Where each mask sits was checked in game. The approach directions — nearest named landmark, compass direction and rough distance — are calculated from the game's own map-marker coordinates (via the open-source Mappalachia database) and cross-checked against the hand-stitched Wasteland Map on this site. Distances are rounded to the nearest 50 m: close enough to find the spot, not survey-grade.

Where the Directions Come From

Landmark positions come from the open-source Mappalachia map-marker database, read for the current game version and cross-checked against the hand-stitched Wasteland Map on this site. Each direction reads from the landmark to the mask spot, so “northeast of Wixon Homestead” means the spot lies northeast of it. Where a spot has no marker of its own, the direction uses the nearest named place instead.
