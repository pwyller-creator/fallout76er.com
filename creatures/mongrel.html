MongrelSpawn Locations

Tracking a Mongrel in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across Ash Heap, Toxic Valley, and Savage Divide. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

Big Bend Tunnel

Ash Heap11 + 4 Alpha

The definitive canine farm — eleven Mongrels plus four Alpha Mongrels inside the tunnel. Clears any Kill a Canine challenge in a single pass.
