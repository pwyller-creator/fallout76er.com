RadtoadSpawn Locations

Tracking a Radtoad in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across Savage Divide, Toxic Valley, and The Forest. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

The Sludge Hole

Savage Divide2 to 3

Just west of Wendigo Cave. Two or three always sit in the waterlogged pit — the one fixed, low-threat spawn, ideal for the Critical Hit daily.

02

Grafton Dam

Toxic ValleyUp to 5

Around five near the water and the dam face. Denser than the Sludge Hole, but Grafton is a higher-level area — come geared.

03

Path to Enlightenment (Event)

The ForestEvent waves

At Landview Lighthouse. Lighting the beacon spawns guaranteed waves of Radtoads at the base — the fastest way to farm a pile of them.

Where does the Radtoad spawn in Fallout 76?
The Radtoad has 3 field-verified spawn locations in Fallout 76, across Savage Divide, Toxic Valley, and The Forest. The most reliable spot is The Sludge Hole in Savage Divide.

Yes — Grafton Dam and Path to Enlightenment (Event) are also documented spawn locations for the Radtoad.
