Mole RatSpawn Locations

Tracking a Mole Rat in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across The Forest and Ash Heap. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

Hillfolk Hotdogs

The ForestUp to 13

South of Sunshine Meadows Industrial Farm. One of the two best Mole Rat farms in the game — well over the five needed for the daily. Stay on the ground or they burrow and vanish.

Welch Station

The Forest3+

A small reliable cluster right at the lodge entrance. Good first stop on a Forest Mole Rat loop before hitting Hillfolk Hotdogs.
