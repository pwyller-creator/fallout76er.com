Flatwoods MonsterSpawn Locations

Tracking a Flatwoods Monster in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across Events, The Mire, and The Forest. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

Invaders From Beyond (Event)

EventsEvent spawn

The Zetan invasion event spawns Flatwoods Monsters across the map at the top of each hour — the only reliable way to find more than one. They only appear when the event is live.
