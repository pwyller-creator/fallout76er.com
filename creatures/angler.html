AnglerSpawn Locations

Tracking a Angler in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across The Mire and Cranberry Bog. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

Gnarled Shallows

The Mire3 guaranteed

East of Abbie's Bunker on the northern tip of the Mire — follow the river north to the pond. Three submerged, disguised as lure weed until you get close. Mirelurks sometimes spawn here too.

02

North of Gnarled Shallows

The Mire3 guaranteed

A second pod sits just north of the Shallows. The two spots together knock out the weekly in a single loop.

03

Thunder Mountain Substation

Cranberry BogUp to 3

A patrolling group of Gulpers often draws Anglers into a fight here — let them soften each other up, then collect both for crafting. Anglers also count toward cryptid challenges.
