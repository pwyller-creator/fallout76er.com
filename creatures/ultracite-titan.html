Ultracite TitanSpawn Locations

Tracking a Ultracite Titan in Appalachia? The Archive logs 1 spawn location for it across Ash Heap. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

Seismic Activity (Event)

Ash HeapNuke boss

Nuke Abandoned Mine Shaft 2 beside Nuka-World on Tour to trigger it. Destroy eleven Ultracite Crystals with melee to summon the Titan, a giant mutated Mole Rat that spits out endless Mole Miners and Ultramites. Drops ultracite scrap and 4-star named weapons.
