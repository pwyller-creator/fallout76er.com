Mega SlothSpawn Locations

Tracking a Mega Sloth in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across The Mire and Cranberry Bog. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

Southern Belle Motel

The MireSmall group

A group hangs from the trees south of the motel. Mega Sloths are passive and do not show on the compass — listen for their low groans or pop Berry Mentats to spot them.

02

Tanagra Town

The Mire1 to 2

Wandering the clearings and hanging from the treeline around town. The Mire is their primary habitat, so this is a dependable secondary stop.

03

Superior Sunset Farm

Cranberry BogChance

They drift into the Bog as well. The weekly is usually Take a Camera Picture of a Mega Sloth — line up the shot before it ambles off, since photo mode does not count.
