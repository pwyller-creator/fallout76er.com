GulperSpawn Locations

Tracking a Gulper in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across The Mire. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

Gulper Lagoon

The Mire4 guaranteed

The definitive Gulper stop. Four always patrol the water right by the fast-travel point. Look up — they perch in the trees and drop on you. Clears the daily in one visit.

02

Dyer Chemical

The Mire2 guaranteed

Two reliably north-northwest of the plant along the river. A quick top-up if the Lagoon was already picked clean.

03

Moonshine Jamboree

The MireEvent wave

Runs near Sunday Brothers' Cabin. The event throws waves of Gulpers, Anglers, and Feral Ghouls at three stills — overkill for a daily, ideal for the weekly, and you bag Anglers on the same run.
