MothmanSpawn Locations

Tracking a Mothman in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across The Forest and The Mire. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

Path to Enlightenment (Event)

The ForestGuaranteed

Mothman has no fixed farm — it is summoned. Fast-travel to Landview Lighthouse when the event is live, fill the lamp with bioluminescent fluid from radtoads and fireflies, and the Wise Mothman spawns at the base once it is lit. The single best way to tick a Mothman challenge — commune with him after for a hefty XP buff.

02

Queen of the Hunt

The MireChance spawn

Starting this event at Hunter's Shack in the Mire can draw a vengeful Mothman. Not guaranteed, but a solid secondary if Encryptid is not active.

MAP PENDING
03

Point Pleasant (Night)

The ForestRare random

The Mothman Museum and surrounding town are the cryptid's lore home, and rare nighttime sightings still occur. Atmospheric, but do not rely on it for a challenge.
