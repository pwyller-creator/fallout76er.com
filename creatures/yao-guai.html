Yao GuaiSpawn Locations

Tracking a Yao Guai in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across The Mire, Savage Divide, and Skyline Valley. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

Dolly Sods Wilderness

The Mire2 guaranteed

One at the entrance road, one en route to the Campground. Private world recommended — these go fast on public servers.

02

Mountainside Bed & Breakfast

Savage Divide2 guaranteed

Follow the railway tracks downhill to the west. Both spawn reliably every reset.

03

High Knob Lookout

Skyline Valley3 guaranteed

Northeast of the lookout at a scenic overlook. Least contested of the reliable spawns.
