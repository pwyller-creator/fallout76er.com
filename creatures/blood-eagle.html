Blood EagleSpawn Locations

Tracking a Blood Eagle in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across Ash Heap. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

Widow's Perch

Ash Heap4–6

Their territory. Dense camp with guaranteed spawns. Combine with a Deathclaw check nearby.

02

The Sludge Works

Ash Heap5–8

Chemical plant thick with Blood Eagles. Elevated positions make this a sniper-friendly run.

03

Rollins Labor Camp

Ash Heap8–12

West of Nuka-World on Tour, where you free Beckett from captivity. The single densest Blood Eagle camp in Appalachia, with good sniping positions across the yard — pair with the Sludge Works next door for a fast weekly clear.
