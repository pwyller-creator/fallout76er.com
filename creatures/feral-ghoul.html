Feral GhoulSpawn Locations

Tracking a Feral Ghoul in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across Cranberry Bog, The Forest, and Toxic Valley. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

Watoga Emergency Services

Cranberry Bog8+

Eight or more crowd the top floor of the old Brotherhood field hospital, with a solid chance at legendaries mixed in. Come geared — these Ghouls run higher-level than most.

02

Charleston Fire Department

The Forest6–10

Guaranteed Ghouls inside and on the grounds. Combine with a lead farm run for double value.

03

Grafton Steel

Toxic Valley6–8

Industrial site with consistent Ghoul density. Watch for the occasional Glowing One in the smelter area.
