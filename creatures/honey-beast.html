Honey BeastSpawn Locations

Tracking a Honey Beast in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across Savage Divide and The Mire. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

South of Middle Mountain Pitstop

Savage Divide3 guaranteed

Three reliably spawn between the Pitstop and Solomon's Pond. The most consistent fixed Honey Beast spot — start a farming loop here.

02

Freddy Fear's House of Scares

The MireUp to 2

In the unmarked cave to the northeast — the same cave the Stings and Things Bloatfly Gland task sends you to. Two Honey Beasts, often with Bloatflies backing them up.

03

Irrational Fear (Event)

The Mire6 to 9

Starts at Raleigh Clay's Bunker. You escort a bee-phobic Mr. Handy and fight off six to nine Honey Beasts — overkill for the daily, perfect for the weekly.
