RadstagSpawn Locations

Tracking a Radstag in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across Savage Divide and The Forest. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

Thomas Farm

Savage Divide4–6

East of Dent & Sons Construction, marked by the big barn. A busted Aerosolizer bleached this whole herd albino-white — the single most reliable Radstag farm on the map.
