Cave CricketSpawn Locations

Tracking a Cave Cricket in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across Savage Divide, The Mire, and Cranberry Bog. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

Lucky Hole Mine

Savage DividePacks

Swarms through the lower levels and the surrounding woods. High Damage Resistance, so bring an energy weapon; ballistics chip away slowly.

02

Tanagra Town

The Mire5 guaranteed

A small cave near the top of the great tree, thick with crickets — the premier Acid farm in the game, over 50 waste acid per clear with a server-hop. Maxed Perception means they are near-impossible to sneak up on — expect to fight, not ambush.

03

Uncanny Caverns

Cranberry BogPacks

Spread through the cave system, densest in the lower levels. A good Bog-side alternative when Lucky Hole is contested.
