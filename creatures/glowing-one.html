Glowing OneSpawn Locations

Tracking a Glowing One in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across Savage Divide and Ash Heap. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

Whitespring Resort

Savage Divide1 reliable

A Glowing One always sits on one of the houses near the northernmost exit — the most dependable fixed glowing ghoul on the map.

02

Camden Park

Ash Heap2 glowing

Two glowing radroaches almost always lurk in the restrooms — the fastest tick for any Photograph a Glowing Creature daily.

03

Blast Zones (Nuked)

Savage DivideMany

Nuking a populated zone like Monongah, Morgantown, or Charleston turns its ghouls into Glowing Ones. The broader Glowing Creature challenge also counts glowing Mole Miners at Mount Blair and glowing Wendigos at Appalachian Antiques.
