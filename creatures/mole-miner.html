Mole MinerSpawn Locations

Tracking a Mole Miner in Appalachia? The Archive logs 3 spawn locations for it across Ash Heap and Savage Divide. Server population and active events shift the odds, but these are the spots the field notes keep returning to.

01

Welch

Ash HeapUp to 10

The premier Mole Miner farm in Appalachia — roughly a dozen patrol the town. Scrap their mining suits and excavation gear, then server-hop to reset.

02

Blackwater Mine

Savage Divide5–8

Interior and exterior spawns. Server-hop to reset quickly. Bring fire resistance — some carry flamer variants.

03

Mount Blair Trainyard

Ash Heap3–5

Sprawling location with multiple spawn pockets. Don't miss the tunnels under the main structure.
