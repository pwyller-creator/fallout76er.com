Well, never passing up a challenge means you get mobbed by a pack of feral ghouls and flanked by some aggressive Rust Raiders at the same time. That's me, balls out with a big sign around my neck saying, "smash em!" My reward? I got to rummage through a Raider trash bin in return for a couple of loads of ultracite .308 shells and a stimpak or two. Another Fallout76er big, ahem, win.
